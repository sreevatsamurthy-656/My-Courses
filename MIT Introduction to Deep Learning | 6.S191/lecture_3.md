# Deep Computer Vision

* Sight and Vision are very important human senses that are used
* Vision in a simple, vague definition is just **To know what is where by looking**
* But a more detailed and exact definiton is to discover from images what is present in the world, where things are, what actions are taking pladce and to predict and anticipate events in the world.
* Deep Learning allows us to understand and comprehend images in a very extensive way in a way similar to vision
* Some applications in this regard are in:
> 1. Robotics
> 2. Mobile Computing
> 3. Accessibility
> 4. Biology and Medicine
> 5. Autonomous Driving
> 6. Facial Detection and Recognition

## What Computers "See"

* Computers see an image as numbers
* An image, in actuality, is a matrix of numbers
* The numbers in an image range from 0 to 255
